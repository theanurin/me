Happy Birthday to My New Blog!

27 Apr 2026

Welcome! Today I am finally celebrating the launch of this blog.

Honestly, I've been putting this off for years. Life, work, and countless other things constantly got in the way. But I decided it was time.

To create this site, I chose the static site generator Eleventy. It was an interesting introduction, and I even did some practical work migrating a structure originally oriented towards Jekyll. It was a useful experience, and I'm happy with the result.

I hope this blog becomes a place where I can share my thoughts, knowledge, and experiences. And I hope it will be useful not only for me but also for you, my readers.

So, welcome to my new online home! There's a lot of interesting things ahead!
